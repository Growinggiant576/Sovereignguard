2. Purpose
2.1. To set out Dubai Health Authority (DHA) `s requirements for Data and Health
Information Protection and Confidentiality in the Emirate of Dubai; in line with the
United Arab Emirates (UAE) laws, and Emirate of Dubai legislative / regulatory
frameworks.
2.2. To assure Entities under jurisdiction of DHA are providing a secure environment for
data management; specifically identifiable health information - also termed “Protected
Health Information” (PHI).
2.3. To outline the requirements and responsibilities of Entities working under jurisdiction
of DHA on Data and Health Information Protection and Confidentiality.
2.4. To ensure the confidentiality of health information and protect the privacy of
community members by providing proper governance for optimal data management

with other Entities or individuals. Compliance against these principles is a key and
failure to comply may lead to regulatory action:

the Data Subject.

must follow the articles in UAE ICT Health Law:
